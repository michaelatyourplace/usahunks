Group Revue

Multiple performers, bigger production

A group revue sends two or more performers for a choreographed, stage-style show. It's the closest thing to a big Vegas production you can host yourself, and it suits large parties or groups who want a full production.

Because you're paying for each performer, expect it to cost several times a solo show. Groups also need a space big enough to work in, and peak-season dates take more planning.

  • Bigger, production-style show
  • Works well for very large groups
  • Much higher cost than a solo show
  • Needs a large rental or event space
3

Male Revue Party Bus

A mobile show around town

A party bus combines transportation and entertainment, with performers on board while the bus loops through town, often with a bar stop. Groups can usually buy individual tickets on a shared bus or book the whole bus privately.

It's a fun option if your group wants to get out and move, but shared buses mean partying alongside other groups, and private buses cost far more than an in-house show.

  • Transportation and a show in one
  • Per-person tickets for smaller groups
  • Shared buses mean strangers on board
  • Private buses are a big step up in price
4

Touring Revue Show

Ticketed nights when a tour comes through

National male revue tours stop in Charleston from time to time, with ticketed shows at local venues. You don't have to host anything, and it's a night out with a crowd.

The catch is timing. Tour dates rarely line up with a specific bachelorette weekend, and the show isn't built around your bride. Check local venue calendars before you plan around one.

  • No hosting or setup
  • Big-crowd, night-out energy
  • Only on tour dates
  • Not personalized to your group

Why a Private Show Wins

For most Charleston groups, a private show is the best value. At $200 for up to 90 minutes, split across the group, it costs less than a round of drinks per person. It also fits around brunch or dinner, and the whole show is about your bride or birthday girl.

Service Areas

Private shows travel across the greater Charleston area, including downtown Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Folly Beach, Isle of Palms, Sullivan's Island, Summerville, North Charleston, Daniel Island, and James Island. Spring and fall weekends fill fastest, so book early when you can.

Booking Tips

Book directly with the entertainer by phone or through their own website, not through an agency that may send someone different. Look for real photos and reviews on platforms like Google or The Knot. Confirm the price, show length, and payment method upfront, and talk through your group's comfort level before the show.
